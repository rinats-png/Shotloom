# Dreamina/CapCut pricing: comparison, NOT a paid adapter

Snapshot research: **2026-11-28**. Dreamina's public pages show different past promotions and account/region-specific credit allowances. Promotional rates that ended in October or before November 28 are **not current provider quotes**. No working programmable Dreamina billing or generation adapter is verified in Shotloom.

1. Dreamina is a web subscription with credits. Its site has listed (US pages) Basic: 1,575 credits/month, $15 renewal; Standard: 3,885 credits/month, $36 renewal; Advanced: 17,600 credits/month, $159 renewal; Ultra: 59,000 credits/month, $520 renewal. Promotions and plans vary; current offers need login/checkout confirmation, including payment method, local taxes, subscription period and credits used **per selected shot and resolution**.
2. A September/October **expired** first-month promotion cited 720p Seedance 2.5 at $0.035 per generated second, and Seedance 2.0 Fast at $0.008/sec. These were **effective within a promotional subscription**, not universal PAYG/API rates.
3. Later Dreamina advertisements have shown **seven or fourteen days of unlimited Seedance 2.5 at 720p** on certain subscription tiers, subject to web-only execution, one concurrent generation, and exact time/plan limits. **Explicitly NOT available via CLI, MCP or Agent Mode.** Do not plug these figures into an autonomous Shotloom provider estimate.
4. Dreamina's informational article about Kling 3.0 does **not** prove the Dreamina workspace offers Kling 3.0 as an actual selectable, billable model. The walkthrough itself uses Seedance 2.0 Mini as the available choice.
5. For a meaningful 15-minute project estimate use a **logged-in Dreamina quote**: model + exact resolution + sound toggle + clip length + reference mode + credits per job, then compute the number of drafts, final attempts, cut overage, and the subscription/credit purchases needed. The number of active paid plan periods matters for longer projects.
6. Dreamina UI functions such as upscale/interpolation and 4K display are not equivalent to official Higgsfield Seedance Draft Finalization; they may change the result or bill credits. Verify output and separately quote the feature.

## Conditional calculator — historical 720p promo ONLY

Assume 900 delivered seconds, 2 rejected previews + 1 accepted = 2,700 generated seconds. Under the **expired 720p Seedance 2.5 $0.035/s** promotional effective rate, the nominal video consumption would be **$94.50**, or **$132.30** after 15% edit overage plus 25% rework reserve (applied additively to base). This is **not a November 28 purchasing quote**, not Full-HD, not proof that the subscription credits can fund the entire job, and excludes final full-resolution rendering. At 720p Seedance 2.0 Fast's expired $0.008/s figure, the corresponding nominal consumption would be **$21.60**, also **not a current live price**.

Useful separate benchmark: an independent November 4, 2026 review reports 37 Dreamina credits per 720p Seedance 2.5 output second. At the published US *renewal* Basic $15/1,575 credits, 900 seconds one pass would take 33,300 credits ≈ **$317.14 nominal credit-equivalent**, three passes 99,900 credits ≈ **$951.43 nominal equivalent**. Actual account procurement costs depend on packs/plan limits; **a subscription alone may not supply enough credits**, and public pages do not establish current exact credits/sec by all resolutions. Recheck in the UI.
